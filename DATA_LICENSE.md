The repository's MIT license applies to original software and project-authored
documentation; it does not replace OSM data licensing. Third-party rights and
notices are retained. Published maps should carry OSM attribution.

## Historical reference: not redistributed

A publicly downloadable IranTracking SQLite reference dated 2024-05-14 was
examined during local preparation. An explicit redistribution license was not
established. Its database, shapes, station records, route–station tables,
record-level crosswalks and reference-derived diagnostics are excluded from
this public repository and archive. They are not offered under MIT or ODbL.
Source links, retrieval metadata and checksums are documented for transparency.

Website-derived hospital evidence fields are also excluded from the public
data. Mapped website tags originally present in OSM are retained as OSM data.
See [provenance](docs/provenance.md) for the distinction between the local
mixed-source release and the public OSM-only distribution.
